Resit — Privacy

How Resit handles your data. Last updated: June 2026.

Resit is a personal expense tracker. The short version: your expenses live on your phone, not on a server. Nothing about you leaves your device unless you choose one of the optional features below.

What stays on your phone

Your expenses, receipt photos you keep, budgets, categories and settings are stored only in your phone's local app storage. We have no account system and no server holding your records. If you uninstall the app or tap Erase all data in Settings, that data is gone.

Reading receipts

On-device (default): when you snap a receipt, your phone reads it locally. The photo does not leave your phone.

Cloud reading (only if you turn it on): if a receipt is hard to read and you have switched on "Smarter receipt reading" and agreed to the one-time prompt, the photo is sent to our reader service to extract the details, and is then discarded. The photo and the extracted text are not stored on the server. To run this, the image passes through Cloudflare (hosting) and Google's vision service (the reader). The only thing the service keeps is an anonymous per-device counter used to prevent abuse — it contains no personal information and no receipt content.

You can turn cloud reading off at any time in Settings; with it off, no photo ever leaves your phone.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no email, no login.
  • No advertising, no selling or sharing of your data.
  • No tracking or analytics of how you use the app.
  • No receipt photos or expense records stored on any server.

Permissions

Resit asks for camera/photo access only so you can capture or pick a receipt. The image is used to read the receipt and nothing else.

Your choices

  • Export / back up: you can export your data to a file you control.
  • Delete: tap Erase all data in Settings, or uninstall the app, to remove everything.
  • Cloud reading: opt in or out anytime in Settings.

Children

Resit is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children.
